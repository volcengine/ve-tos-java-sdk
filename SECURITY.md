# Security Policy

## Security and privacy

If you discover a potential security issue in this project, or believe you may have found one, please notify the ByteDance security team through our [security center](https://security.bytedance.com/src) or [vulnerability reporting email](mailto:src@bytedance.com). Please do not create a public GitHub issue.

We will assess the vulnerability based on the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS 3.1). The security team will keep you updated on key progress and may request further information or guidance from you. You are welcome to contact us through the email address or website above to ask questions or discuss disclosure matters.

To protect our customers, please do not publish or share information about the vulnerability in a public forum, or publish or share user data, until the vulnerability has been remediated and affected users have been notified. The time required for remediation depends on the severity of the vulnerability and the scope of its impact.

Individuals, companies, and security teams may wish to publish security advisories on their own websites or other forums. Please contact us through the email address or website above before publication to discuss what information can be disclosed and to coordinate the disclosure timeline.

## Bug Bounty Reward

For information about ByteDance's bug bounty program, visit the [ByteDance Security Response Center](https://src.bytedance.com/home).
